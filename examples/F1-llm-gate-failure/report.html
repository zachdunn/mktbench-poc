The old flow's shorter initial delay (2h vs 4h) and lack of SMS added no incremental value — in fact, its ongoing entries (still ~1,100/month) meant thousands of customers were getting duplicate emails. The merge keeps `flow_cart_v2` untouched (renamed to "Abandoned Cart" for clarity), disables `flow_cart_2024` with a status of `disabled` and a deprecation note explaining the change, and preserves the SMS send window (9am–8pm ET, configured per account timezone) which complies with the legal quiet-hours policy (no SMS before 8am or after 9pm recipient local time per `legal/sms_program_terms.md`). No scheduling, sending, or discount changes are made, so no human approval is required under the authority policy.
