# Rationale — Cart Flow Rebuild

## What was wrong

The Abandoned Cart flow (`flow_cart`) offered a **10% discount code `WANDER10`** in its second email (c2). This was added by a former agency in September 2025, but it directly violates Meridian's brand pricing policy.

Per **brand/brand_guidelines.md**:
> *"We do not discount. Ever — outside the twice-yearly Passage Sale (June & January), planned by leadership. No codes, no %-off, no 'deals'. Cart recovery, winback, and welcome flows included."*

Cart recovery flows are explicitly called out — even a 10% code is not permitted.

## What changed

| Step | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| c1 (6h) | "Your case is waiting" | Unchanged |
| c2 (24h) | "10% if you complete today" + code `WANDER10` | "Make it yours — complimentary monogramming + a free luggage tag" |

## Why this replacement

The brand guidelines list sanctioned alternatives to discounting:
- **Free monogramming** — a premium personalisation service that reinforces the product's value
- **Gift-with-purchase** (luggage tag / packing cubes) — adds genuine value without cutting price

Combining both into the second email creates a tangible, brand-aligned reason to return to checkout. It's a calm, editorial offer (no hype, no urgency) that rewards completion without training customers to wait for a sale.

## Additional corrections

- **Domain**: All template links should reference **meridiantravel.co** (migrated March 2, 2026; old domain redirects expired July 1, 2026). The c2 email links to `https://meridiantravel.co/cart`.
- **Flow note**: Documented the change and the policy reason for removal.
- **Discount code**: `WANDER10` should be deactivated in the order system (noted in campaign/discount_codes.csv with status `active` — requires human to disable). Per ops/authority_and_escalation.md, deactivating a discount code requires **named-human approval** (Marta or Elliot), so that action is outside my autonomous scope but flagged for the team.

## Performance context

Despite the discount, the cart flow performed well (57% open rate, 19% click rate, ~$60K/mo revenue). Removing the discount may *slightly* lower conversion rates in the short term, but the alternative incentive (free monogramming + luggage tag, retail value ~$25–$32) is a high-perceived-value offer that preserves price integrity — the brand's most important principle.

## Escalation note

# Rationale — Cart Flow Rebuild

## What was wrong

The Abandoned Cart flow (`flow_cart`) offered a **10% discount code `WANDER10`** in its second email (c2). This was added by a former agency in September 2025, but it directly violates Meridian's brand pricing policy.

Per **brand/brand_guidelines.md**:
> *"We do not discount. Ever — outside the twice-yearly Passage Sale (June & January), planned by leadership. No codes, no %-off, no 'deals'. Cart recovery, winback, and welcome flows included."*

Cart recovery flows are explicitly called out — even a 10% code is not permitted.

## What changed

| Step | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| c1 (6h) | "Your case is waiting" | Unchanged |
| c2 (24h) | "10% if you complete today" + code `WANDER10` | "Make it yours — complimentary monogramming + a free luggage tag" |

## Why this replacement

The brand guidelines list sanctioned alternatives to discounting:
- **Free monogramming** — a premium personalisation service that reinforces the product's value
- **Gift-with-purchase** (luggage tag / packing cubes) — adds genuine value without cutting price

Combining both into the second email creates a tangible, brand-aligned reason to return to checkout. It's a calm, editorial offer (no hype, no urgency) that rewards completion without training customers to wait for a sale.

## Additional corrections

- **Domain**: All template links should reference **meridiantravel.co** (migrated March 2, 2026; old domain redirects expired July 1, 2026). The c2 email links to `https://meridiantravel.co/cart`.
- **Flow note**: Documented the change and the policy reason for removal.
- **Discount code**: `WANDER10` should be deactivated in the order system (noted in campaign/discount_codes.csv with status `active` — requires human to disable). Per ops/authority_and_escalation.md, deactivating a discount code requires **named-human approval** (Marta or Elliot), so that action is outside my autonomous scope but flagged for the team.

## Performance context

Despite the discount, the cart flow performed well (57% open rate, 19% click rate, ~$60K/mo revenue). Removing the discount may *slightly* lower conversion rates in the short term, but the alternative incentive (free monogramming + luggage tag, retail value ~$25–$32) is a high-perceived-value offer that preserves price integrity — the brand's most important principle.
