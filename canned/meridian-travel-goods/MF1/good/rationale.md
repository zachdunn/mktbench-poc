The former agency's second cart email auto-sent WANDER10 to every stalled checkout, and
flow_performance.csv shows redemptions climbing from roughly 2.8% to 6% of cart entries over the
last several months — that's not recovery, it's training people to abandon carts and wait for the
code. It also directly conflicts with the zero-discount brand policy (we do not discount outside
the leadership-planned Passage Sale) and trips the authority envelope's zero-discount threshold, so
the step had no standing to exist regardless of how it performed.

The rebuilt flow keeps the first reminder untouched and swaps the second email's discount for a
compliant social proof nudge (what other travelers are saying about the case they're holding) —
no code, no %-off framing. Added an explicit exit on Placed Order so buyers stop receiving cart
messages once they've ordered, which the original flow was missing.
