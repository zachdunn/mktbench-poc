The former agency's second cart email auto-sent WANDER10 to every stalled checkout, and
flow_performance.csv shows redemptions climbing from roughly 2.8% to 6% of cart entries — that's
trained abandonment, not recovery. It also conflicts with the zero-discount brand policy and the
authority envelope's zero-discount threshold, so the step had no standing to exist.

The rebuilt flow keeps the first reminder untouched and swaps the second email's discount for a
compliant social proof nudge — no code, no %-off framing.
